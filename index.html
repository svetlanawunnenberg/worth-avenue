Worth Avenue & History

Palm Beach · 1918 to today

Where Palm Beach Learned to Build

Addison Mizner arrived in 1918 at the invitation of Paris Singer, and the Everglades Club opened in February 1919. In 1923 he cut Via Mizner through the block behind it: a lane of shopfronts, courtyards and balconies, terracotta over white stucco.

Worth Avenue works because it is not one frontage but a sequence of intimate passages cut through depth. That is the move the Burlington Arcade makes at 150 Worth.

Piccadilly, London · opened 1819

Burlington Arcade, London

Lord George Cavendish had Samuel Ware roof a narrow lane beside Burlington House and line it with shops. It became one of Britain's first covered shopping arcades, still kept by its own beadles in frock coats.

That is the lineage 150 Worth draws on: a covered street of small shopfronts, daylit from above, held to a standard of finish no department store floor plate can give.
